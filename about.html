About MyLPU.online

"MyLPU.online is an independent student-focused platform created to make campus-related information easier to discover. Our goal is to bring useful information about hostel mess schedules, food menus, shops, cafeterias and campus facilities together in one convenient place."

Key Principles

  • Accurate Data: Directly integrated from the verified Hostel Mess spreadsheet.
  • Independent: Run for students, by students.
